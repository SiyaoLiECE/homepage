About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU). Previously, I was an Assistant Professor in the Electrical Engineering Department at the University of Alaska Anchorage in Spring 2024 and a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Telecommunication Systems at Technical University of Berlin from 2022 to 2023, advised by Prof. Giuseppe Caire. I received my Ph.D. (2021) in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois Chicago, advised by Prof. Daniela Tuninetti and Prof. Natasha Devroye. Prior to this, I obtained an M.S. degree (2016) in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois Chicago.
